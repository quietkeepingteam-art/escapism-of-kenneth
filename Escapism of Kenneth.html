What's being tested

SpeedRaw pace. Cheap to lean on, easiest for a predictive patrol to intercept.
AwarenessHow far ahead he spots a patrol — early warning bought cheap.
UnpredictabilityPatrols aim at where he's heading. A wandering line defeats that.
AdaptabilityHow fast he abandons a blocked plan for a new route when a gate drifts shut.
StealthWhether he actively ducks toward a moving wall to break a patrol's line of sight, rather than just running.
ResolveHis grit. Cuts how long a detention stuns him, and raises how many hits he can take before a run is finally over.
ConcentrationHow hard he stays locked on the goal itself. High concentration pulls him more directly at the target and makes him less likely to peel off into a detour.
Self-controlWhether he overreacts to a threat that's still distant. High self-control means he only commits to evasive action once something is genuinely close.
